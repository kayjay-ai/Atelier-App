4. Billing Cycles and renewals

  1. Plans are paid one Billing Cycle at a time. Plans do not renew automatically; the Store pays for each new cycle in the app.
  2. Every Plan payment starts a new Billing Cycle on the day the payment is confirmed, with that Plan's full credit allotment.
  3. Renewing the same Plan, or moving to a lower-priced Plan, becomes available in the final days of the current Billing Cycle, as shown in the app.
  4. If a Billing Cycle ends without renewal, the Store may keep using Top-up Credits during a grace period (currently 7 days). After the grace period the Store is suspended until a Plan is paid for. Suspension does not remove Top-up Credits.

5. Credits

  1. Plan Credits expire at the end of each Billing Cycle. Unused Plan Credits do not carry forward and are not refunded.
  2. Top-up Credits do not expire. They are used only through consumption and remain available through renewals and upgrades, for as long as the Store account exists.
  3. Plan Credits are always used before Top-up Credits.
  4. One Credit is consumed for each successful use of a credit-based feature. If a generation fails on our side, the Credit is returned automatically.
  5. Credits have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, and cannot be transferred between Stores or to another business.

6. Upgrades and plan changes

  1. Upgrades are charged at the full price of the new Plan, with no proration and no adjustment for the remaining days or credits of the current cycle.
  2. An upgrade starts a new Billing Cycle on the day of the upgrade, with the new Plan's full credit allotment. Unused Plan Credits from the previous cycle expire at that moment; Top-up Credits are kept.
  3. The app shows, before payment, what will happen to the Store's existing credits.
